# Default design reflexes

For each line the question is the same: **chosen or default?** A reflex is not a
fault in itself. It is a fault when nobody decided it for this product. If you keep it,
write the reason in one sentence. If you cannot, replace it.

## Colour

- Harsh gradients on backgrounds or buttons.
- Violet-to-blue gradient, and violet with black: the signature of generated pages.
- Neon accents on dark backgrounds with no link to the brand.
- Bland pastels that avoid every decision.
- A different colour for each section, like a rainbow.
- Pure white (`#fff`) background everywhere with pure black text. Try a tinted
  neutral instead and check contrast again.

## Shape and depth

- A drop shadow on every card and button.
- The same corner radius on everything (buttons, cards, inputs, images).
- Floating icons down the side of the page.
- A decorative stripe or line above a title or card.
- Bento grids because they are fashionable, not because the content is uneven.
- Dot grids or grid-line backgrounds behind the hero.
- Glowing orbs or blurred blobs behind content.
- Glassmorphism on every panel.
- A fake terminal window with three coloured dots to show code or a command.

## Layout and content patterns

- Exactly three feature cards in a row with an icon each.
- Exactly three pricing tiers with the middle one highlighted.
- Checkmark bullets on every list.
- Sparkle icons to mean "AI".
- A bouncing arrow pointing down under the hero.
- **No real product demo**: no screenshot, recording or live sample of the thing being
  sold. This is the costliest reflex, because visitors cannot judge what they cannot see.
  Show the real product, even small.
- Missing loading skeletons: the page jumps or shows blank areas while data loads.

## Motion

- A hover animation on every element.
- A scroll fade-in on every block.
- An animated gradient on the headline.

## Type and identity

- Emoji used as icons.
- Inter, Geist or Space Grotesk used by default. Pick a family for a reason (tone,
  language coverage, licence) and pair it with one other.
- A logo that is only the product name typed in the body font.
- A script font for "personality".
- A "made with" badge from the generator left in the page.
- A deployment subdomain (`something.vercel.app`) shown as the final address.

## Honesty

- Fake testimonials, user counters or figures. Remove them or replace them with real,
  sourced ones.
- Buttons and links that go nowhere (`href="#"`, no handler).
- A vague headline ("The future of work, reimagined"). It should say what the product
  does, for whom.

## How to use this list

1. Run it on the direction in Stage 1 and again on the finished page in Stage 5.
2. Count the reflexes that remain defaults. More than three, or any of the two
   marked as costliest or dishonest, means go back to Stage 1.
3. Note the answers in the decision log.
